今仔記 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

「今仔記」是一句話日記 App。我們的原則很簡單：你寫的東西只屬於你，留在你的手機裡。

我們不收集任何資料

  • 不需要註冊或登入，沒有帳號。
  • 不會把你的日記、心情或任何個人資料傳送到我們或第三方的伺服器。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具、不使用 Cookie 追蹤你。

資料存放在哪裡

你寫的日記、心情分類與 App 設定（例如主題色、提醒時間）都只存在這支手機的 App 儲存空間裡。刪除 App 時，這些資料會一起刪除。

如果你在手機的系統設定中開啟了 Android 的「備份」功能，Android 系統可能會依照你的設定，把 App 資料一併備份到你自己的 Google 帳號。這是由 Android 與 Google 處理，我們無法存取這些備份。

匯出與匯入備份

只有在你按下「匯出備份」時，App 才會產生一個備份檔，並開啟系統的分享選單，由你決定要存到哪裡（例如雲端硬碟或傳給自己）。匯入時也只會讀取你選擇的檔案。

通知

每日提醒是手機本機排程的通知，不經過任何伺服器。你可以隨時在 App 的設定或手機的系統設定中關閉。

日曆（選用）

如果你在設定中打開「日曆」，App 會請求讀取手機日曆的權限，在你寫一句時列出那個時間的行程，讓你把記錄連到行程。日曆資料只在手機上讀取，不會上傳，App 也不會新增或修改你的行程。連結時只會在記錄裡存下行程的名稱與時間，跟其他記錄一樣只存在手機裡。你可以隨時關閉這個功能，或在手機的系統設定中收回權限。

進階版購買

進階版透過 Google Play 購買，付款與帳單資料由 Google 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。App 只會向 Google Play 確認這個 Google 帳號是否買過進階版，結果存在手機裡。

兒童

本 App 不以兒童為對象，也不會收集任何人的個人資料。

政策變更

如果這份政策有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期。

Jinaki Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Jinaki (今仔記) is a one-line diary app. Our principle is simple: what you write belongs to you and stays on your phone.

We collect no data

  • No sign-up or login; there are no accounts.
  • Your entries, moods and any personal information are never sent to our servers or to any third party.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies.

Where your data is stored

Your entries, mood categories and app settings (such as theme color and reminder time) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If you have turned on Android's backup feature in your device settings, Android may include this app's data in a backup to your own Google account, according to your settings. That backup is handled by Android and Google; we cannot access it.

Exporting and importing backups

The app creates a backup file only when you tap "Export backup", then opens the system share sheet so you decide where it goes. Importing only reads the file you choose.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled locally on your device and does not go through any server. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on "Calendar" in Settings, the app asks for permission to read your device calendar so it can list events around the time of an entry and let you link the entry to an event. Calendar data is read on your device only and is never uploaded; the app never adds or changes your events. When you link an event, only its title and time are saved with the entry, on your device like all other entries. You can turn this off at any time or revoke the permission in your device settings.

Premium purchase

Premium is purchased through Google Play. Payment and billing information is handled by Google; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account has purchased Premium and keeps that result on your device.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above.
